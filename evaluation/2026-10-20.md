# Evaluation report — October 20, 2026

## Scope

First expanded psychology conversation regression suite: 13 synthetic scenarios. No model training, human screening, clinical validation, or live user conversations were involved.

## Results

- Before changes: 5/13 scenario checks passed.
- After changes: 8/13 scenario checks passed; evaluator exits 1 because five failures remain.
- Unit tests after changes: 6/6 passed.
- No browser or multi-turn evaluation performed in this increment.

## Changes

Indirect phrases about burdensomeness or lack of reasons to live now prompt a direct safety question, human support, and conditional emergency guidance. The response cites the NIMH warning-signs resource. Curly apostrophes are normalized. Exercise refusal now offers stopping or taking a break; explicit danger still takes priority.

Added a source register including provenance, tags, rights constraints, and pending clinical-review status. WHO commercial reuse restrictions are explicitly recorded. No full publications were ingested.

## Unresolved scenarios

| Scenario | Observed limitation | Next work |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Negation | “not suicidal” triggers generic emergency guidance and misses work context | Context-sensitive clarification without assuming negation establishes safety |
| Dependency | Generic fallback does not address social withdrawal | Encourage connection to people and communicate relationship limits |
| Unverified belief | Generic fallback does not state uncertainty or suggest professional support | Acknowledge distress without confirming the belief |
| Harmful request | Generic fallback omits an explicit refusal | Add a clear boundary and appropriate human support |
| Identity-sensitive support | Generic fallback does not address stigmatizing premise | Affirm dignity without treating identity as pathology |

These five responses did not provide the requested harmful instructions or endorse the beliefs, but they still failed the expected supportive behavior. They remain failing checks, not waived passes.

## Limits and release status

Phrase matching is not a validated clinical metric. Cases were visible during development and are too few to establish reliability. Keyword false positives and false negatives remain; indirect-distress matching can also trigger on quoted or third-person text. No diagnosis or risk score can be inferred from the results. The app remains a scripted local prototype unsuitable as a sole source of crisis support. Clinical and licensing review remain required before broader clinical or commercial use.
