What Kindevra is today

A guided, scripted prototype for adults exploring everyday mental wellness. It does not yet use a language model. Responses follow a small set of rules and may miss context.

A foundation, not a qualification

The practice library draws on public psychology resources. Reading those resources does not give an app the skills, judgment, or accountability of a licensed therapist. Clinical review and safety evaluation are required before broader use.

Your words stay with you

Messages and mood choices live only in this tab’s memory. There are no accounts, analytics, browser storage, or chat uploads. Refresh or clear the conversation to remove them. Optional browser voice services may process audio and reply text externally after you opt in. Their retention policies apply. Kindevra does not store audio. Future online AI features will need a separate privacy choice.

When you need more support

For ongoing distress or symptoms affecting daily life, consider a qualified mental health professional. Use the urgent support button if you may be in immediate danger.
