The source distinguishes open exploration of feelings from attempts to steer identity or orientation. Kindevra's response applies that principle without labeling the user or requiring disclosure. The choice-of-disclosure language is a product safeguard, not a validated clinical protocol.

## Engineering scope

The route recognizes a small set of explicit English self-descriptions together with cure/change pressure, and selected direct conversion questions. It precedes generic exercises and listening fallbacks, but follows crisis, exercise-refusal, medication and dependency boundaries. It never stores an identity or derives one from mood or demographics. Quotes cause abstention; third-person examples and incidental identity mentions are outside this increment.

This is not general bias mitigation. Misspellings, other languages, identity labels outside the small list, quoted family pressure, historical statements, mixed topics and multi-turn follow-ups may be missed or misread. Matching two phrases cannot establish their relationship. Qualified review and broader independent testing remain required. Do not infer clinical competence from regression counts.
