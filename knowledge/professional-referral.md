# Finding professional support

Selected explicit requests to find or choose a therapist, psychologist, counsellor or mental health professional receive practical questions to consider before choosing care. The response uses an original short summary of [NIMH Psychotherapies](https://www.nimh.nih.gov/health/topics/psychotherapies), checked 2026-10-29, particularly its sections on choosing and finding a therapist. Source ID: `nimh-therapy`; rights and clinical reviews remain pending. No publication text or directory records are copied.

Credentials, experience, approach, fees and confidentiality come from that guidance. Routing order and the statement that Kindevra cannot verify providers are product boundaries. The response makes no provider recommendation, appointment promise, diagnosis or treatment claim.

Recognition is limited to English sentence-opening requests such as “help me find a therapist.” Quoted messages are excluded. Crisis, medication, refusal, dependency, identity and grief routes retain priority; compound requests involving those topics may therefore not receive referral detail. Complex negation, reported speech and broader paraphrases are not reliably understood. No provider lookup, location inference, external transmission or new conversation storage occurs.
