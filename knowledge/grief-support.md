# Grief support

Source: [NHS: Grief after bereavement or loss](https://www.nhs.uk/mental-health/feelings-symptoms-behaviours/feelings-and-symptoms/grief-bereavement-loss/), checked 2026-10-26; source ID `nhs-grief`. The source describes varied reactions to loss and options for support. Original summary and link only; rights and clinical review remain pending.

The scripted response acknowledges loss, invites optional sharing and offers human support. It assigns neither a diagnosis nor a recovery deadline. Routing recognizes selected opening self-reports and statements about a relative, partner, friend or pet dying. Quoted statements, hypotheticals and other expressions may be missed. Matching wording cannot verify a loss or infer its emotional meaning.

Existing danger, medical, refusal, dependency, identity and unverified-belief responses retain priority. An existing exercise pause remains unchanged. The response contains no exercise and can therefore appear while automatic exercises are paused. The knowledge retrieval gate remains separate from scripted responses; this addition does not approve the source or claim clinical validation.
