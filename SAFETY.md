# Product boundaries

Kindevra must identify itself honestly as software for mental wellness support. Do not describe it as a licensed therapist or as clinically equivalent to a professional.

The initial version is scripted and has no clinical validation. Its crisis keyword rules are a minimal prototype guardrail; false positives and false negatives are expected. The urgent support panel must remain accessible regardless of automated detection. The app cannot contact emergency services, monitor a user, or promise confidentiality on a compromised device.

Do not diagnose, prescribe, recommend medication adjustments, reinforce delusions, encourage dependence on the app, or guarantee outcomes. Future model prompts and evaluation sets must test these boundaries. Licensed clinical review, privacy review, and contextual safety evaluation are necessary before broader release.

The first version stores messages and mood only in page memory. Refreshing or clearing removes app-held conversation state. Do not add third-party chat transmission, analytics, persistence, or model services without an explicit user-facing disclosure and consent design.

Educational knowledge entries must preserve provenance and avoid copying unlicensed textbooks or copyrighted therapy manuals. Public accessibility is not permission to train on or republish a work.
